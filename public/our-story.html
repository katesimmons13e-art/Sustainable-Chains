Our Story

At Sustainable Chains, we’re on a mission to make transparency the new normal. We started with one simple idea: what if every brand could clearly show where their products come from, and every customer could see it?

Our platform lets brands log their supply-chain points and showcase verified locations on a public, interactive map. For consumers, that means you can explore trusted brands, discover your next favorite, and make informed choices; instantly seeing which companies align with your values.

Built with SQLite, Leaflet maps, and powered by Stripe, Sustainable Chains combines cutting-edge tech with a commitment to sustainability. We’re here to empower brands, guide conscious consumers, and make ethical shopping easier than ever.
